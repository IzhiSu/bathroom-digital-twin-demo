Bathroom Digital Twin Case Gallery

This page documents case-based bathroom datasets used to support environment digitization, spatial interpretation, and reconstruction in the Bathroom Digital Twin framework. Each case acts as a structured evidence unit connecting field documentation with digital model development.

Significance of Case Collection

  • Establish a case-grounded basis for the Bathroom Digital Twin. The purpose of collecting real bathroom cases is to provide empirical support for scene construction, rather than relying only on an idealized or synthetic bathroom layout.
  • Capture real spatial and contextual conditions. Real case data provide the spatial and contextual basis for constructing bathroom scenes that reflect actual layouts, fixture arrangements, movement constraints, and safety-relevant environmental details.
  • Prepare for future AI-assisted risk detection. As more cases and annotations are accumulated, the case gallery can support future exploration of AI-assisted methods for detecting potential environmental hazards or risk factors, such as constrained movement areas, high-risk fixture arrangements, wet zones, obstacle locations, and possible impact surfaces.
  • Build a scalable case archive for digital twin development. The gallery is not only a documentation archive, but also a preparatory dataset structure for future intelligent scene understanding, cross-case comparison, and risk-informed bathroom digital twin development.

How to read this gallery

Each case is organized using the same structure: case overview, floor plan, 3D point cloud, contextual images, reconstruction model in Unity, and a short integration summary. Click the gray case header row to expand or collapse each case.

Case-based inputs

Floor plan, spatial documentation, point cloud data, and contextual images collected for each case.

Digital outputs

Reconstructed bathroom scenes in Unity used as the environmental basis for DT interaction and risk analysis.
