Detailed Steps

Step 1. Case selection and interview setup

A real bathroom case is selected as the basis for early-stage investigation and prototype development.

  • Define the target bathroom environment.
  • Prepare the interview scope.
  • Clarify required materials for later digitization.

Step 2. Field interview and contextual documentation

Interview and observation are used to connect lived use experience with observable bathroom conditions.

  • Record bathroom use and perceived inconvenience.
  • Observe movement constraints and wet-area conditions.
  • Capture visual records of fixtures and spatial context.

Local condition

Step 3. Raw data collection

Image records, interview materials, scan data, and model-related files are collected as complementary evidence.

  • Image data for spatial and contextual documentation.
  • Recording data for interpretation of use and perception.
  • Scan-derived data for geometric reconstruction.

Step 4. Data preprocessing and organization

Raw materials are organized by data type to support traceable reconstruction and interpretation.

  • Organize Image, Recording, and Building_Model folders.
  • Select representative field images.
  • Prepare spatial data for cleaning and reconstruction.
File structure
Preprocessing

Step 5. Environment digitization

Spatial evidence is transformed into digital representations of the bathroom environment.

  • Prepare point cloud or scan-derived geometry.
  • Generate digital model representations.
  • Identify safety-relevant objects and zones.
Point cloud
Digital model

Step 6. Integration into the Bathroom Digital Twin

Processed case data are integrated into a DT-ready scene for later behavior playback, contact-event analysis, and safety-oriented interpretation.

  • Use the digitized bathroom layout as the scene basis.
  • Link object-level spatial information to safety semantics.
  • Prepare the case for interaction-aware analysis.
Output: a traceable case package linking field evidence, spatial digitization, and digital twin scene construction.
